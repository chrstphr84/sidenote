▎ SideNote Help

How notes, highlights, and the margin work.

Overview

SideNote lets you attach comments to highlighted text on any web page, shown in a margin beside the page — like comments in Google Docs. Notes are saved locally and reappear when you return to the page, even after closing the tab.

Turning it on

  • Click the SideNote toolbar icon to open the popup.
  • All pages is the master switch.
  • This page turns SideNote on or off for the current page.
  • Pages that already have notes turn on automatically when you visit them.
  • The toolbar icon shows a badge with the number of open notes on the current page.

Adding a note

  • Select text on the page. An Add note button appears next to the selection.
  • Click it — the text is highlighted and a card opens in the margin. Type your note and Save.
  • Click a highlight to jump to its note; click a note's quote to scroll back to the highlighted text.

The margin

  • The margin can sit on the Left, Right, or Both sides — set the default in Settings, or per page from the popup.
  • Open and close the margin with the tab on the edge of the page; closing it never deletes notes.
  • With both margins shown, use a note's ⇄ button to move it to the other side.
  • Each note can be edited, resolved (✓), or deleted (🗑).

Highlights that move

  • SideNote remembers the highlighted text along with the words around it, so it can re-find the spot after a reload.
  • If the page changes so the text no longer exists, the note is kept and flagged not found on page in the margin.

All notes

  • The All notes page (from the popup or Settings) lists every page you've commented on, in its own tab.
  • Open a page by its link, or remove pages individually, by selection, or all at once.

Theme

  • Light, Dark, and Auto themes are supported. Auto follows your operating system and updates live.
  • The theme applies to the popup, margin, and all extension pages.
